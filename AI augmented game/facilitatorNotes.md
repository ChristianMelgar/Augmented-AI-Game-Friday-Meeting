# Facilitator notes — The Augmented Consultant Game

This file holds workshop copy that **does not appear in the app UI** but may be useful when running a session: real-engagement timing, deliverable definitions, brainstorm seeds, and “wow the client” examples.

The game reads phase config from `stepsConfig.js` only. Edit this file for facilitator reference; edit `stepsConfig.js` for what players see.

---

## Phase 1 — Project initiation

**Timing in a real engagement:** Week 0–1  
**Typical effort:** Usually happens informally over coffee. Rarely given dedicated time. Often lives in one person's head.

### Typical deliverables

| Deliverable | What it is |
|-------------|------------|
| **Hypothesis** | A structured set of initial beliefs about what is driving the network problem — ranked by likelihood and grounded in publicly available data, analogues, and early client signals. |
| **Impact case** | A high-level quantification of the value at stake — setting out what success looks like financially and operationally, and why the engagement is worth the investment. |
| **Case for change** | A compelling narrative for why the status quo is unsustainable — combining financial performance, market dynamics, and strategic pressure into a clear burning platform. |
| **Project approach & scope** | A structured document defining what the engagement will and will not cover, the analytical workstreams, methodology, team roles, and key milestones. |
| **Governance & ways of working** | Proposed meeting cadence, decision-making structure, escalation paths, and working norms between the client and consulting team across the engagement. |
| **Project plan & mobilisation** | A detailed week-by-week plan covering workstream sequencing, resource allocation, data requests, and the critical path to final delivery. |
| **Workshop & engagement setup** | Design and logistics for key alignment moments — kickoff sessions, steering committee introductions, and early stakeholder engagement to build trust and momentum. |

### Brainstorm seeds (optional prompts)

- How could AI help you build a structured hypothesis tree before you've seen any client data?
- Could AI scan the financials and press material and generate a first set of hypotheses ranked by likelihood?
- What would it look like to walk into the kickoff with an AI-generated POV the client reacts to — instead of blank questions?

### Wow the client — example

Presenting a structured hypothesis tree in the first client meeting — ranked by likelihood, grounded in industry analogues — before you've done a single interview. That's something clients rarely see.

## Phase 2 — Building the fact-pack

**Timing in a real engagement:** Weeks 2–5  
**Typical effort:** Typically 2 analysts, 2–3 weeks. The most time-intensive phase of most ops engagements.

### Typical deliverables

| Deliverable | What it is |
|-------------|------------|
| **Fact-pack** | The consolidated baseline document — bringing together all quantitative and qualitative findings into a single, agreed picture of the current state that all stakeholders can work from. |
| **Data & analysis** | Structured analytical outputs covering site economics, capacity utilisation, product flows, and cost-to-serve — cleaning and interpreting raw data into decision-ready insights. |
| **Stakeholder & qualitative insights** | Synthesis of interviews and workshops — capturing leadership perspectives, operational realities, and areas of misalignment across the organisation. |
| **Benchmarking & external perspectives** | Comparison of NordicFoods' performance against industry peers and best-in-class operators — providing external reference points to contextualise the data and build the case for change. |

### Brainstorm seeds (optional prompts)

- How could AI accelerate turning raw interview recordings into a structured synthesis — with themes, contradictions, and quotes?
- What could AI do with a messy 30-row data extract that would normally take an analyst two days to clean and interpret?
- How could AI help you spot the patterns in the data that the client themselves hasn't noticed?

### Wow the client — example

A fact-pack that synthesises 14 interviews, flags contradictions across stakeholders, maps them to your hypothesis tree, and identifies the three things the data says that nobody expected — produced in a day rather than a week.

## Phase 3 — Cracking the analysis

**Timing in a real engagement:** Weeks 5–8  
**Typical effort:** Senior consultant + specialist, 2–3 weeks. The phase that determines the quality of the answer.

### Typical deliverables

| Deliverable | What it is |
|-------------|------------|
| **Design criteria** | An agreed set of principles and requirements that any viable solution must meet — covering cost, service levels, sustainability, risk, and strategic fit — used to evaluate scenarios objectively. |
| **Scenario definition** | A structured set of 3–5 distinct network configurations worth evaluating, each with a clear strategic logic, scope, and set of underlying assumptions. |
| **Scenario modelling & simulation** | Quantitative build-out of each scenario across cost-to-serve, capex requirements, service level impact, and operational feasibility — turning strategic options into numbers. |
| **Scenario evaluation** | A structured comparison of all scenarios against the design criteria — making the trade-offs explicit and providing a clear basis for decision-making. |
| **Sensitivity & risk analysis** | Testing how robust each scenario is under different assumptions — volume shifts, cost changes, implementation delays — and identifying the key risks associated with each option. |
| **Recommendation** | A clearly articulated point of view on which scenario to pursue, with the supporting rationale, key conditions for success, and an honest account of the trade-offs. |
| **Business case** | A CFO-ready financial model quantifying the value at stake, investment required, payback period, and key assumptions — structured to support board-level decision-making. |
| **Implementation & transition plan** | A phased roadmap for moving from current state to the recommended future state — covering workstreams, sequencing, resource requirements, governance, and key dependencies. |

### Brainstorm seeds (optional prompts)

- How could AI help you generate network scenarios you might not have thought of — based on the site data and your hypotheses?
- Could AI help structure the evaluation criteria and populate a scenario comparison faster than a manual Excel build?
- How could AI help you stress-test your recommendation — finding the weaknesses before the CFO does?

### Wow the client — example

Evaluating six scenarios instead of three because AI compressed the build time. A sensitivity analysis the client didn't expect. A recommendation that's been stress-tested against every likely CFO objection before you walk into the room.

## Phase 4 — Landing it with the client

**Timing in a real engagement:** Final 2 weeks  
**Typical effort:** Senior consultant, 1 week. Often under-prepared because the analysis took longer than planned.

### Typical deliverables

| Deliverable | What it is |
|-------------|------------|
| **Pre-read presentation** | A concise document sent to steering committee members ahead of the session — summarising the recommendation, key findings, and the decisions required, so the meeting can focus on alignment rather than information transfer. |
| **Steer-co deck** | The main presentation used in the steering committee — structured to take decision-makers from context through analysis to recommendation, with a clear ask and supporting evidence. |
| **Aligning stakeholders & securing approval** | The facilitation of key conversations before, during, and after the steering committee — managing objections, building coalitions, and ensuring the recommendation lands with the authority needed to move to implementation. |

### Brainstorm seeds (optional prompts)

- How could AI help you simulate difficult stakeholder conversations — so you walk in having already rehearsed every likely objection?
- How could AI help you craft a recommendation narrative that lands differently for the CFO, the COO, and the Head of Commercial?
- What formats for presenting the recommendation could AI help you produce that you wouldn't normally have time to build?

### Wow the client — example

Walking into the steering committee having rehearsed every objection with an AI playing the sceptical CFO. A pre-read personalised to each member. A recommendation that's been iterated ten times in an afternoon instead of once.
